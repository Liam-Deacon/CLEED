CLEED
=====

**Computational Low Energy Electron Diffraction Package**

CLEED is a comprehensive software suite for LEED-IV structural analysis,
enabling researchers to determine surface atomic structures by comparing
experimental and theoretical low-energy electron diffraction intensity curves.
